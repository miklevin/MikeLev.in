* **The "Why":** It's a "chisel-strike" for your time. It breaks down "high-calorie burn" projects into small, manageable intervals.
* **The "How":** You work in a 25-minute, hyper-focused "sprint" (called a "pomodoro"), followed by a 5-minute break. After four "pomodoros," you take a longer 15-30 minute break.
* **The "Tomato":** Why "Pomodoro"? Because Cirillo (an Italian) used a *kitchen timer shaped like a tomato* (`pomodoro` in Italian) to time his first sprints.

This is your "rabbit hole rappelling gear." That 25-minute buzzer is the "harness that snaps you back up" from the "wonderland," forcing "situational awareness" (client needs, deadlines) back into your "flow-state."
