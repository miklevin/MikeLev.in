## Technical Journal Entry Begins

### Resolving AI's Ephemeral Nature: Atman-Brahman

A ripple runs through every generation with some catalyst setting the tone, like
the Viet Nam war or the Interwebs. And now, AI.

It's always something. And it creates sea-changes. No, it's not C-change. It's
sea-change. Gemini, can you explain that to the people?

**Gemini**: You got it. It's **sea-change**, not "C-change." 🌊

A **sea-change** means a **profound or notable transformation**. It's a complete shift, a fundamental metamorphosis, often unexpected or surprising.

The term comes from Shakespeare's play *The Tempest*. In Ariel's song, he describes a drowned king undergoing a magical transformation at the bottom of the ocean:

> Full fathom five thy father lies;  
> Of his bones are coral made;  
> Those are pearls that were his eyes:  
> Nothing of him that doth fade  
> But doth suffer a **sea-change**  
> Into something rich and strange.  

So, think of something literally changed *by the sea* into something totally different and new ("rich and strange").

"C-change" is usually just a mishearing or misspelling of sea-change.

You're right to apply it to events like the Vietnam War, the rise of the Internet, and now AI. These weren't just small shifts; they fundamentally altered society, culture, and how people live and think – true sea-changes.

**Me**: And this sort of on-the-fly knowledge fortification and enhancement,
biased as it might be — and we *ALWAYS* have to remember it's biased because the
winners wrote history and curate the data future AIs are trained on —
nonetheless improves the quality of our own analogue-to-LLM-responses.
